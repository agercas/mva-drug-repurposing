# Senolytic clearance with dasatinib and quercetin

## Claim as stated

Dasatinib with quercetin, a senolytic combination (senolytics kill senescent cells, which are cells in permanent growth arrest), is "validated in BubR1 progeria" and extends lifespan by 24% to 27% in BubR1 mice by clearing p16-positive senescent cells. BubR1 is the spindle checkpoint protein encoded by *BUB1B*, and progeria is premature ageing. About seven public proposals include it and four consider and reject it.

## Evidence for

- Genetic ablation of p16-positive cells (the INK-ATTAC transgene, which lets a drug trigger death of p16-positive cells) delayed adipose, skeletal muscle and eye features in BubR1 progeroid mice (PMID [22048312](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22048312/); abstract and full text read).

- Dasatinib with quercetin killed senescent cells in culture and was tested in aged, irradiated and *Ercc1* progeroid mice (PMID [25754370](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25754370/); abstract read). It was also tested in a small open-label study in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (PMID [30616998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30616998/), n = 14, feasibility endpoints; abstract read).

## Evidence against

1. **The BubR1 result used genetic ablation (INK-ATTAC),** with phenotyping in female mice on a mixed genetic background. Adipose, muscle and eye benefit appeared; heart and aorta phenotypes and overall survival were not substantially rescued (PMID [22048312](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22048312/); full text read).

2. **Dasatinib with quercetin was never tested in a BubR1 model.**

3. **The 24% to 27% gain was reported in wild-type mice by genetic ablation** of p16-positive cells from one year of age: median lifespan rose 27% in mixed-background and 24% in C57BL/6 mice (PMID [26840489](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26840489/); full text read).

4. **Dasatinib's label** reports clastogenicity (chromosome breakage) in vitro, rodent tumours at about 60% of human exposure and growth warnings in children.

5. **Loss of p53 or p21 shortens maximum lifespan in H/H mice** (BubR1 hypomorphic mice; Wang-Allison p = 0.023 and 0.003; PMID [23602569](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23602569/); full text read), so removing p16-positive cells in a cancer-prone host is untested.

## Conclusion

Genetic clearance is a mouse result with partial benefit. The drug combination is untested in a BubR1 model, and its label carries findings that count against it in a child with cancer predisposition.

## Same weakness in this report

The report's candidates also rest partly on mouse and cell-line results from outside mosaic variegated aneuploidy, and none has been tested in a BubR1-deficient human cell.
