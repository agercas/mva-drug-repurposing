# Mitotic-kinase, spindle, HSP90 and proteasome inhibitors

## Claim as stated

Aneuploid cells, which carry an abnormal chromosome number, depend on stress pathways. Inhibitors of the spindle checkpoint kinase MPS1 (monopolar spindle 1), Aurora B, WEE1, the kinesin KIF18A, the chaperone HSP90 (heat shock protein 90; inhibitor 17-AAG, 17-allylamino-17-demethoxygeldanamycin), the proteasome (the protein-degrading complex; bortezomib) or autophagy (the cell's recycling of its own components; chloroquine) therefore kill them selectively.

The same proposals often assert that HSP90 inhibition is contraindicated because missense BubR1 (the spindle checkpoint protein encoded by *BUB1B*, with one amino acid changed) depends on HSP90. About 22 candidate listings fall in this family.

## Evidence for

- AICAR (5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleoside) killed mouse fibroblasts trisomic for chromosomes 1, 13, 16 or 19 (carrying an extra copy) by p53-mediated apoptosis. AICAR and 17-AAG showed efficacy against aneuploid human cancer cell lines, and chloroquine was also identified as aneuploidy-selective (PMID [21315436](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21315436/); abstract only).

- Aneuploid human cells were more sensitive to proteasome inhibition in diploid-versus-aneuploid cell models, and aneuploidy levels were associated with response to proteasome inhibitors in patients with multiple myeloma (PMID [39247952](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39247952/); abstract read).

- In the 2010 allele study, geldanamycin lowered the levels of two kinase-region mutant proteins (PMID [20516114](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/), Fig 3C).

## Evidence against

1. **The same class has opposite directions of effect.** HSP90 and proteasome stress is the hazard in the stability literature and the basis for killing aneuploid cells in the selectivity literature. A proposal that relies on the selectivity literature inherits the stability hazard as well.

2. **The HSP90 contraindication rests on one experiment:** tagged I909T and L1012P proteins in U2OS cells, 5 hours of geldanamycin (an HSP90 inhibitor) or MG132 (a proteasome inhibitor), with steady-state protein as the readout and half-life unmeasured (full text read, Fig 3C).

3. **Aneuploid cancer cells were less sensitive than diploid cells to short-term exposure to several spindle checkpoint inhibitors** and became more sensitive to long-term inhibition (PMID [33505028](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33505028/); abstract read), which complicates any single-dose reading of selectivity.

4. **The paper that proposals cite for the HSF1 (heat shock factor 1) link tested 17-AAG and used MG132 to induce the heat-shock response.** Bortezomib was not among its agents (PMID [25205676](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25205676/); full text read).

5. **The proteasome-inhibitor result rests on few clones.** The bortezomib comparison used two pseudo-diploid and two multiply trisomic RPE-1 clones (five experiments; p = 0.044 and 0.016), and the cancer-line comparison contrasts the top and bottom aneuploidy quartiles of 203 lines (p = 0.040) (PMID [39247952](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39247952/); full text read).

6. **The nearest reported benefit of a mitotic-kinase inhibitor is a combination effect.** The BUB1 kinase inhibitor BAY 1816032 was synergistic or additive with taxanes, ATR (ataxia telangiectasia and Rad3-related) inhibitors and PARP (poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase) inhibitors, and induced chromosome missegregation with low-dose paclitaxel (PMID [30429199](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30429199/); abstract read). The paper covers the BUB1 kinase inhibitor in combinations; MPS1, Aurora or WEE1 inhibitors and single-agent killing of aneuploid cells lie outside its scope.

7. **These classes raise chromosome missegregation** (PMIDs [33505028](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33505028/) and [30429199](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30429199/); abstracts read), which this report treats as the wrong direction in a host that already missegregates (Our inference).

## Conclusion

Benefit and contraindication are both untested in a BubR1 or mosaic variegated aneuploidy (MVA) system. The classes fail the safety screen on direction of effect.

## Same weakness in this report

The report's exclusion of cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) and spindle agents also rests on cancer-model and abstract-level evidence. The HSP90 hazard carries the same one-experiment limit.
