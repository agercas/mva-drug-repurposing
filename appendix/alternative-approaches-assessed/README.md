# Alternative approaches assessed

Ten approaches from public proposals and the literature are assessed here. Each file gives the claim as stated, the evidence for, the evidence against with its location, and a conclusion. The claims come from public proposals for this disease and are cited by claim, with the proposals' authors and repositories left unnamed.

## Selection

1. An approach is included if it appears in public proposals or in the literature for mosaic variegated aneuploidy (MVA) or for deficiency of BubR1, the spindle checkpoint protein encoded by *BUB1B*, and is not among the candidates in sections 3 and 4.

2. The counter-evidence comes from primary papers read in full where the file says so, and from abstracts where it says so. A weak counter-source is described as weak.

3. Where this report's own record shares the same weakness, the file says so.

4. A candidate drug that appears in several proposals is counted once per approach, so a count gives how often an approach was proposed.

## Summary

Across 164 candidate listings in 27 public proposals that name candidates, 66 are rejected (wrong direction of effect, failed safety screen, not approved or a refuted premise) and 66 rest on a mechanism that is only partly supported (55) or unsound (11). The most common weakest step, shared with this report's own candidates, is the assumption that a drug alters clearance or folding of the child's missense protein; that effect is unmeasured.

The table gives each approach, the claim as stated and the conclusion. NMD is nonsense-mediated decay, JAK is Janus kinase, mTOR is the mechanistic target of rapamycin, HSP90 is heat shock protein 90, MEK is mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase, ADAR is adenosine deaminase acting on RNA, AICAR is 5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleoside and tRNA is transfer RNA.

| Approach | Claim as stated | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [Readthrough and NMD inhibition](readthrough-and-nmd-inhibition.md) | Readthrough or NMD inhibitors restore full-length BubR1 from the stop allele | Tested in other genes only; the inserted residue may differ from wild type |
| [Chaperones and proteostasis](chaperones-and-proteostasis.md) | Arimoclomol or 4-phenylbutyrate rescue misfolded BubR1 | Data come from other proteins; cell doses of 100 to 400 µM are about 15 to 100 times the peak plasma level at twice the labelled dose |
| [Senolytics](senolytics.md) | Dasatinib with quercetin clears damaging cells in BubR1 disease | Tested only by genetic ablation; drug untested in a BubR1 model |
| [mTOR and autophagy](mtor-and-autophagy.md) | Rapalogs (rapamycin analogues), metformin or AICAR rescue or harm BubR1-deficient tissue | Direction of effect unresolved; the one test of metformin is negative in culture |
| [Interferon and JAK blockade](interferon-and-jak-blockade.md) | An inflammatory signature in MVA justifies JAK inhibition | Signal seen in one adult with variants in a different gene |
| [Mitotic-kinase, spindle, HSP90 and proteasome inhibitors](mitotic-and-stress-pathway-inhibitors.md) | Aneuploid cells are selectively killed, or the same agents are contraindicated | Opposite directions of effect are asserted for the same agents; both untested in a BubR1 system |
| [MEK inhibitors](mek-inhibitors.md) | Trametinib or selumetinib remove aneuploid cells that depend on MEK signalling | Unsupported; the approach removes aneuploid cells, no BubR1-deficient result exists, and the selumetinib label records an aneugenic finding |
| [Kinase inhibitors chosen by docking](docking-selected-kinase-inhibitors.md) | Approved kinase inhibitors dock at the lysine that the N1002K change creates | Unsupported; the poses sit about 28 Å from the residue, and the leads are antiproliferative drugs |
| [Sequence-level rescue routes](sequence-level-rescue-routes.md) | Base editing, ADAR editing, a leucine suppressor tRNA, exon-17 skipping or gene replacement rescue the stop or missense allele | The sequence derivations hold; each route lacks three measurements, and the suppressor tRNA yields one tenth to one twenty-fifth of reversion per edited cell |
| [Zero drug counts in drug-target databases](zero-drug-counts-from-target-databases.md) | A count of zero drug edges shows no repurposable drug | The count needs a base rate, and link prediction failed its positive control |
