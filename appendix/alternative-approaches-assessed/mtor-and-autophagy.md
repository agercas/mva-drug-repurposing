# mTOR inhibition and autophagy modulation

## Claim as stated

Rapalogs (rapamycin, also called sirolimus, and its analogue everolimus) rescue tissue deficient in BubR1, the spindle checkpoint protein encoded by *BUB1B*, by dampening hyperactive mTORC1 (mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1, a growth-signalling kinase complex). Metformin, AICAR (5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleoside) and autophagy inducers (autophagy is the cell's recycling of its own components) act through related pathways. Rapalogs appear in 13 candidate listings and the metformin/AICAR family in 13.

## Evidence for

- mTORC1 signalling is raised in muscle of monoallelic *Bub1b* mice (one of the two gene copies affected) with sarcopenia, the age-related loss of muscle (PMID [31738183](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31738183/); abstract and partial full text read; the paper has a corrigendum, PMID [33136097](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33136097/), whose content was not read). The evidence is correlative: two mice by western blot, three per genotype by RNA sequencing, and no perturbation run.

- In a *Drosophila* model of chromosomal instability, heterozygosity for *mTOR* and feeding of 40 µM rapamycin lowered the number of aneuploid neural stem cells (neuroblasts) in larval brains (PMID [41820377](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41820377/); full text read, Fig. 6D and Supplementary Fig. 6C).

- Rapamycin and the PI3K (phosphoinositide 3-kinase) inhibitor LY294002 suppressed a raised anaphase-bridge index (a marker of chromosome missegregation) in rat intestinal epithelial and human colon cancer cell lines in which CDX2 was suppressed or Akt was activated (PMID [14625550](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14625550/); abstract read; the setting is CDX2 loss in colon tumours).

## Evidence against

1. **Raised mTORC1 was seen in monoallelic mouse muscle.** The study reports no mTORC1 result for the L1002P compound line and ran no rapamycin experiment (PMID [31738183](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31738183/)).

2. **The only mammalian BubR1 rapamycin report is a one-sentence null result from unpublished data:** in preliminary studies, BubR1 progeroid mice fed a rapamycin diet developed age-related phenotypes at similar rates to controls (PMID [23602569](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23602569/); full text read).

3. **In the fly paper, genetic interventions that increase autophagy did not rescue Prospero-positive cell number or brain size** (Fig. 6E, Supplementary Fig. 6C). The authors suggest that inhibition of TOR (target of rapamycin) or non-physiological autophagy might harm proliferation of neuroblasts with extensive aneuploidy (PMID [41820377](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41820377/)).

4. **Rapamycin induced chromosome missegregation** in human lymphocytes and lymphoblasts, hamster embryonic fibroblasts and yeast (PMID [9914383](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9914383/); abstract read).

5. **Metformin did not specifically sensitise trisomy-7 colonic epithelial cells** (PMID [22890317](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22890317/); full text read, Supplementary Figure S8a, not opened). The paper reports that AICAR was four times more cytostatic (growth-inhibiting) to the trisomy-7 line than to its diploid counterpart. It also reports that the original study of aneuploidy-selective compounds saw no similar specificity with metformin (PMID [21315436](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21315436/), abstract read; the statement about it is second-hand).

6. **Transplant labels** for sirolimus and everolimus carry boxed warnings on lymphoma and other malignancies (see Other sources); the everolimus oncology label (Afinitor) carries none.

7. **Sirolimus raised micronuclei weakly in human lymphocytes,** at its highest concentration only, 50 ng/mL (PMID [15859941](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15859941/); abstract read), against trough levels of about 5 to 15 ng/mL [UNVERIFIED: trough range quoted from the project notes].

## Conclusion

The direction of effect is unresolved, part of the evidence against rests on abstracts, and the one test of metformin is negative in culture.

## Same weakness in this report

None of the candidates in this report is a rapalog, metformin or AICAR. A direct test in a BubR1-low human cell would decide the direction of effect.
