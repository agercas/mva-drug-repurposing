# Chaperone and proteostasis agents

## Claim as stated

Arimoclomol amplifies the heat-shock response (the cell programme that raises chaperones, proteins that help other proteins fold) and HSP70 (heat shock protein 70), rescuing misfolded BubR1, the spindle checkpoint protein encoded by *BUB1B*. Sodium or glycerol phenylbutyrate (4-PBA) acts as a chemical chaperone for the missense protein (BubR1 with one amino acid changed). Both act on proteostasis, the cell's control of protein folding and turnover. About eight public proposals include a chaperone agent and two reject it.

## Evidence for

- Kinase-region missense alleles in the 2010 allele study lowered abundance (5 to 10 times as tagged proteins in HeLa and U2OS cells; PMID [20516114](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/); full text read). The two tested alleles regained function when overexpressed to wild-type level. Mutant protein fell further under inhibition of HSP90 (heat shock protein 90, a chaperone). This fits a folding or stability defect, which the paper infers from a 5-hour drug experiment.

- Arimoclomol induced HSP70 family chaperones and improved refolding and lysosomal activity of glucocerebrosidase in Gaucher patient fibroblasts (PMID [30497978](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30497978/); full text read) and has been given to patients in other diseases (PMIDs [34418116](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34418116/), [38782015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/)).

## Evidence against

1. **No BubR1 readout exists** in any cited arimoclomol paper (PMIDs [30497978](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30497978/), [34418116](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34418116/), [38782015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/)).

2. **Doses in the cited cell work were 100 to 400 µM** (PMID [30497978](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30497978/)), about 15 to 100 times the peak plasma concentration at twice the maximum recommended dose. The label reports a steady-state peak plasma concentration of 2,090 ng/mL at twice the maximum recommended dosage, which is 6.7 µM for arimoclomol (313.8 g/mol) or 4.1 µM if the figure refers to the citrate (505.9 g/mol); 100 / 6.7 = 15 and 400 / 4.1 = 97. The levels are total plasma concentrations.

3. **Clinical results were borderline or negative.** In Niemann-Pick type C (50 patients, 12 months) the primary endpoint favoured arimoclomol, with a difference of −1.40 on a severity scale where lower is better (95% confidence interval −2.76 to −0.03, so the interval nearly reaches zero; P = 0.046; PMID [34418116](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34418116/), abstract read). In amyotrophic lateral sclerosis the phase 3 trial was negative (Combined Assessment of Function and Survival 0.51 versus 0.49, P = 0.62; treatment-related adverse events 65% versus 52%, discontinuation 16% versus 5%; PMID [38782015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/), abstract read).

4. **Heat-shock-response biology in aneuploid cells is a different system.** Raised HSF1 (heat shock factor 1, the regulator of the heat-shock response) counteracted the HSP90 expression and protein-folding deficits of aneuploid human cells, which carry an abnormal chromosome number (PMID [25205676](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25205676/); abstract read). The paper does not test BubR1.

5. No paper on 4-PBA was read for this report.

6. Glycerol phenylbutyrate carries rodent tumour findings at about 3 to 5.5 times paediatric exposure on its label (see Other sources).

7. Misfolding of the N1002K protein at reachable doses is unmeasured.

## Conclusion

The approach has partial support from the abundance-limited class of kinase-region alleles. As a drug hypothesis it is unsupported, because no experiment links a chaperone agent to BubR1.

## Same weakness in this report

A folding defect of the N1002K protein is an unmeasured premise for any chaperone idea. The measurement of abundance, half-life and chaperone dependence of the missense allele tests it.
