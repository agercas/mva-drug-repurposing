## 7.1 Disclaimer

> **This work is a research hypothesis, not medical advice.**
>
> This analysis was produced for the "Rare Disease, Real Kid: MVA Hackathon
> 2026". It is **not** a diagnosis, not a treatment recommendation, and not a
> substitute for clinical judgement. The drug candidates discussed are
> **hypotheses proposed for further investigation** by qualified researchers and
> clinicians. None has been tested in Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy. No claim is
> made that any of them is safe or effective for this child or any other person.
>
> Any clinical decision must be made by the treating clinical team, with the
> family, on the basis of evidence appropriate to that decision.
